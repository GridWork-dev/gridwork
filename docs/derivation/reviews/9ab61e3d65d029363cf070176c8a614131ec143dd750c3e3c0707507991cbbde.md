their outcome. `publish.rs` adds a first-publish readiness handshake and splits a
locally-refused control (continue) from a transport failure (reconnect). `main.rs`
wires the new start-manager and reaper tasks into startup/shutdown. `lib.rs` is a
doc-only rewrite of the crate's "boundary that remains" section into what shipped.
